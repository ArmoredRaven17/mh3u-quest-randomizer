How to use

1. Pick a quest type & level. Choose Village, Port, Arena or ALL, then a From / Up to Level range. In ALL the range runs Village 1★ → Port 8★ → Arena 8★.

2. Narrow the pool with the sidebar filters:

  • Quest Filters — uncheck a category to drop every quest in it. Capture, Multi Monster and Repel sit under Large Monsters and go with it. Keys Only? keeps just the quests an Urgent Quest needs. Quest Type Filters switch individual stars off everywhere (Arena is off to start with).
  • Monsters — uncheck a monster to drop every quest that targets it.
  • Weapons — uncheck a weapon class to stop it being rolled.
  • Challenges — add your own conditions (e.g. "No Armor"), each with a chance %, that roll alongside the quest. Set how many can apply per quest, and toggle each one on/off. Chaos Mode hands the on/off decision to the RNG each roll.

3. Hit Randomize. You get a quest and a weapon class, plus any challenges that rolled. Arena quests give you one of the quest's five loaner sets instead, since that's what you fight in.

⚙ Theme — change the color theme, or put a flat blue/green behind the card for chroma-keying.

↗ Stream view — pops the result card out into its own window on a green background, updating with every roll, for capturing in OBS.

Export / Import — save your filters and challenges to a file, or load them back.

Filters, challenges and theme are saved between visits. Reset Filters restores the defaults and keeps your challenges.

Reset Filters?

Quest filters, levels, monsters and weapons go back to their defaults. Your challenges are kept.

MH3U Quest Randomizer

An unofficial, non-commercial quest randomizer for Monster Hunter 3 Ultimate — it rolls a quest and a weapon for when you can't decide what to hunt. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by Capcom.

Every quest — its name, star, locale, objective and monsters — and every Arena loaner set is read from the game's own quest files, and the monster, quest and weapon icons are the game's own. Everything was extracted from a personally owned copy of the game; no game files are redistributed. Downloadable Event and Challenge Quests aren't on the cartridge, so they aren't in the pool. The monster classes in the Monsters filter are assigned by hand.

Monster Hunter 3 Ultimate and all of its names, data and icons are © CAPCOM CO., LTD. The UI textures and the MHFU font are shared with the same author's MHGU fan apps, and the app itself is a port of the MHGU Quest Randomizer.
